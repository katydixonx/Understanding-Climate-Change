# Data 

Raw data for the audit of Bastin et al. (2019) Understanding Climate Change from a global analysis of city analogues 
PLOS ONE 14(7): e0217592. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0217592

## Source
Supporting Information S2 and S3 Files from the published correction:
Bastin et al. (2019) Correction, PLOS ONE 14(10): e0224120. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0224120

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `pone_0224120_s002.csv` | 2050 bioclimatic variables per city (RCP 4.5, mean of 3 GCMs) |
| `pone_0224120_s003.csv` | Present-day (1970–2000, WorldClim 2) bioclimatic variables per city |

Note: the files are not labelled as present day or 2050 by the authors. This was worked out by comparing temperatures (see notebook).
